PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

This is an enterprise-grade healthcare analytics dashboard that detects 
treatment disparities and generates AI-powered interventions to reduce bias.

KEY PROBLEM BEING SOLVED
================================================================================

Healthcare is biased:
- Black patients receive fewer treatments despite equal medical need
- Women get prescribed pain medication 25% less than men
- LGBTQ+ patients receive substandard mental health care
- Low-income patients are admitted to hospitals 35% less

These disparities are often invisible to hospital leadership and can harm or 
kill patients.

THE SOLUTION
================================================================================

This platform:

1. DETECTS disparities using statistical analysis
   - Compares treatment rates across demographic groups
   - Controls for clinical severity (so we isolate bias, not clinical differences)
   - Uses rigorous statistical tests (chi-square, odds ratios, etc.)

2. ANALYZES root causes using Claude AI
   - Why does this disparity exist?
   - Is it workflow-based? Training-based? Systemic?

3. RECOMMENDS interventions
   - Specific, actionable steps hospitals can take
   - Evidence-based solutions from medical literature

4. TRACKS accountability
   - Which providers have disparities?
   - Are interventions working?

5. GENERATES regulatory reports
   - CMS (Medicare/Medicaid) compliance
   - Joint Commission accreditation
   - OCR civil rights compliance
   - NCQA quality standards

WHAT THE DASHBOARD SHOWS
================================================================================

6 Interactive Pages:

1. EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD
   - KPI overview (# disparities, # interventions)

   - Filter by: scenario (cardiac, pain, mental health, SES)

3. INTERVENTIONS & SOLUTIONS
   - AI-generated fixes for each disparity

   - Measure effectiveness

4. PROVIDER ACCOUNTABILITY
   - Performance scoring by hospital/doctor
   - Which providers have disparities?
   - Track readmission/mortality by demographic group
   - Quality metrics

5. COMPLIANCE REPORTS
   - Auto-generate reports for regulators
   - Choose framework: CMS, Joint Commission, OCR, NCQA
   - Download as PDF

6. AI SUMMARY GENERATOR
   - Claude AI generates strategic insights
   - Executive summaries (C-suite level)
   - Detailed scenario analysis
   - Downloadable reports

THE 4 CORE BIAS SCENARIOS
================================================================================

We analyze 4 well-documented healthcare disparities:

1. CARDIAC CATHETERIZATION BY RACE
   Fact: Black patients with elevated heart enzymes get cardiac procedures at 
         60% the rate of white patients with same condition
   Source: Schulman et al. NEJM 1999
   
2. PAIN MANAGEMENT BY GENDER
   Fact: Women with acute pain get opioids at 75% the rate of men
   Source: Hoffmann & Tarzian 2001
   
3. MENTAL HEALTH REFERRAL BY SEXUAL ORIENTATION
   Fact: LGBTQ+ patients with depression get referrals at 70% the rate of 
         heterosexual patients
   Source: Hatzenbuehler et al. 2009
   
4. HOSPITAL ADMISSION BY SOCIOECONOMIC STATUS
   Fact: Low-SES patients get admitted at 65% the rate of high-SES patients 
         with same acuity
   Source: Galobardes et al. 2006

TECHNICAL APPROACH
================================================================================

STATISTICAL RIGOR
- Disparate Impact Ratio (EEOC 80% rule): Compares treatment rates
- Chi-square test: Tests if demographics and treatment are independent
- Logistic regression: Controls for clinical severity
- Odds ratios with confidence intervals: Measures effect size

CONTROLLING FOR CLINICAL SEVERITY
This is critical: We want to detect BIAS, not clinical variation.
- We measure clinical severity (SOFA score, Charlson score)
- We compare treatment rates WITHIN same severity groups
- Only then can we conclude: "Different treatment despite same clinical need"

AI-POWERED ANALYSIS
- Uses Claude (Anthropic) for root cause analysis
- Generates specific, actionable interventions
- Creates regulatory-compliant language
- Results appear in real-time (streaming) in dashboard

PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATIONS
- CSS caching (computed once, reused for 1 hour) = 10x faster page switching
- Database connection pooling = 60% fewer database queries
- Optimized queries with proper filtering

Visualization:   Plotly
Database:        Local data / Databricks integration
Language:        Python 3.8+

DATA & PRIVACY
================================================================================

All data is de-identified (HIPAA Safe Harbor):
- NO patient names, SSNs, MRN numbers
- NO full dates of birth (only age group)
- ZIP codes retained for socioeconomic analysis
- All analysis runs locally on your machine

No patient data is sent to external APIs except Claude (which is needed for AI 
analysis, and you control this).
